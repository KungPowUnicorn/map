About

Welcome to Mika's Adorable Playhouse! This is a cozy little collection of educational games for toddlers and kids, where learning looks suspiciously like playing.

Expect lots of tapping, plenty of giggling, and at least one very enthusiastic poke at the screen. Grown-ups are welcome too, but you may have to wait your turn!
